Evaluation Metrics

These validation measurements were used to compare the candidate machine-learning models.

Accuracy

The percentage of all predictions that were classified correctly.

Precision

How many items predicted as threats were actually threats.

Recall

How many actual threats were successfully detected by the model.

F1-Score

A balanced measurement combining precision and recall.
